Important

Saving a schedule does not itself trigger a capture from this browser. The supplied CrabTrack_WeightNode_ScheduledHistory.ino firmware contains the capture routine and reads each node's schedule from Firebase. Flash compatible firmware to each weight node, set the correct compartment number, and verify Serial Monitor shows [HISTORY] Saved daily capture.

History is stored at /crab_monitor/weight_history/C01/YYYY-MM-DD through C10. This page expands every child record for the selected date, shows the exact Firebase path, and refreshes automatically every 15 seconds while enabled.
